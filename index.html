About SPSMUN 3.0 · 2026

SPSMUN is the Model United Nations conference hosted by Satluj Public School, Sector 4, Panchkula. After SPSMUN 2.0 brought together 500+ delegates across the world's committees, the third edition — “The Era of Impact” — returns in 2026, bigger and bolder.

Delegates step into the role of diplomats, debating global issues, drafting resolutions and sharpening their public speaking, research and negotiation skills.
